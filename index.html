Nana Kyeri Beyifour

Nana Kyeri Beyifour is a respected spiritual guide and traditional herbalist from Manso Abore in the Ashanti Region of Ghana. Born into a lineage of healers, he was trained from a young age in the sacred arts of herbal medicine, spiritual consultation, and ancestral wisdom.

His mission is simple yet profound: to offer compassionate guidance to those seeking clarity in matters of the spirit, family, relationships, and personal well-being — always with respect, confidentiality, and humility.
